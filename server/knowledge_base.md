## What Cavalier Flooring Systems Does

Cavalier Flooring Systems is a commercial flooring contractor based in Virginia. We install commercial flooring on commercial projects — new construction and renovation — for general contractors, architects, facility managers, and end-user owners.

We do not take single-family residential work. Multi-family projects (apartment buildings, condominiums, student housing, senior living, dorms) are commercial and are a core part of our business.

## Where We Work

We serve Charlottesville, Central Virginia up to Fredericksburg, and the Tidewater region (Virginia Beach, Norfolk).

## What We Install

We install five flooring categories:

- **LVT (luxury vinyl tile)** — durable, low-maintenance, replaced VCT in most commercial settings
- **VCT (vinyl composition tile)** — still used in cost-sensitive spaces; requires stripping and waxing
- **Carpet tile** — common in office, education, multi-family amenity, and corridor work
- **Sheet vinyl** — typical in healthcare and other hygiene-driven environments
- **Ceramic tile** — restrooms, lobbies, wet areas

Removing the existing floor on a renovation is sometimes part of our scope and sometimes handled by a separate demo contractor — it depends on the project.
